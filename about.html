About KIDAI

A conceptual redesign focused on making civic-service information easier to understand and navigate.

Who we are

KIDAI is a frontend UI/UX concept created for an IEEE hackathon. It explores how a complex public-service website can be organized around citizen goals instead of organizational structure.

Design principle

People should not have to understand how an organization is structured before they can complete a task.

Vision & mission

Make important public-service information clearer, calmer and easier to access.

Leadership

Prototype information section with progressive disclosure.

Our offices

Prototype information architecture for location and office resources.

Careers

Conceptual career-information entry point.

Transparency & accountability

Provide clear pathways to relevant public information.

Legal framework

Organize legal resources so users can understand what each document is for.

Aadhaar dashboard

A dedicated informational entry point can summarize public metrics without overwhelming the homepage.
